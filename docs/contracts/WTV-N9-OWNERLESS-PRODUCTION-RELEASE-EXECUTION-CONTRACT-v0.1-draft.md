## 16. STOP rules

Stop and return to Human when any of the following is true:

- requirements／DoD／QA identity or meaning changes;
- repository、target or QA／Production correlation is ambiguous;
- QA／Production credential crossover or service-role application binding／import／fallback／use is detected;
- provider access would require CLI login fallback;
- an unexpected or non-correlated Production row appears;
- M11、release Head、deployment or operation artifact identity mismatches;
- creator privilege exceeds adopted requirements;
- Data API、Authentication、Firewall or another mutation outcome is ambiguous;
- an unauthorized operation occurred or Production retry would be required;
- `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` remains unresolved;
- a blocking review finding remains;
- the Execution Plan would exceed adopted requirements; or
- public launch、N12、N13 or merge is mixed into N9 execution.

Ordinary Local syntax、fixture、test or formatting defects are not Human STOP conditions when they can be corrected without changing authority、target、scope or live operation count.

## 17. Escape hatch

If the planned method cannot safely meet the Goal:

1. stop the affected operation and preserve current state;
2. do not retry or substitute another provider path;
3. report the failed assumption、missing fact and actual operation count;
4. propose the smallest safe change to the responsible Contract、Execution Plan、canonical authority or operation;
5. obtain the required Human re-adoption／authorization; and
6. resume only from a newly valid boundary.

The escape hatch never permits scope expansion、emergency Production mutation、credential workaround or permission inheritance.
